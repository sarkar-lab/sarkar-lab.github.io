I am an **Assistant Professor** in the newly established [<span style="text-decoration:underline; color:#0055A4;font-weight:600;">College of Connected Computing</span>](https://computing.vanderbilt.edu/) and a faculty member of the [<span style="text-decoration:underline; color:#0055A4;font-weight:600;">Center for Computational Systems Biology</span>](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/computational-biology/) at **Vanderbilt University**. Our research focuses on **deciphering the spatial architecture of complex diseases such as cancer** using **machine learning and artificial intelligence**. We develop computational [methods]({% post_url 2024-03-02-method-dev %}), sometimes it involves inventing entirely **new inference algorithms**, other times we **refine and accelerate existing ones** — pushing them to keep pace with the next generation of sequencing and imaging technologies. <span style="font-weight:600;">✨ Stay tuned for our latest discoveries on</span><br><a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7vu64WsAAAAJ&hl=en&inst=10749622055976013885" target="_blank" style="font-weight:700; text-decoration:none; color:#1a73e8;">Google Scholar 📖</a>
